How It Works

Built for real-world location sharing.

A short code is generated from your coordinates. For true cross-device sharing, the website creates a URL containing the coordinates, so the receiver can open the exact point without needing your browser's local storage.

01

Browser asks for location permission.

03
Share
Send the share link by WhatsApp, SMS, or email.
04
Open Map
The receiver sees the exact location on the map.
🔒
Privacy & safety

This demo does not send your GPS data to a custom backend. Saved locations remain in your browser's local storage. A shared URL contains the coordinates, so treat a location link as sensitive information and only send it to people you trust.
